Welcome to Clymax Signature

A three-storey hotel of rooms and suites, finished in marble and warm light, with a pool, two lounges and our own grill under one roof.

Every room has a king bed, air conditioning, a smart TV, a work desk and its own bathroom. Wi-Fi runs 24 hours, power stays on day and night, the forecourt is gated and guarded, and our driver can meet you at the airport.

What we offer

Everything you need without leaving the hotel.

Free Wi-Fi

24 hours, everywhere

Free in every room, both lounges, the restaurant and by the pool. Ask the front desk for the password at check-in.

Uninterrupted power

Light and air conditioning, day and night

The power stays on around the clock, so air conditioning, lights, Wi-Fi and hot water keep running.

Outdoor pool

8:00 – 20:00

Hotel guests swim free, one guest per room. Visitors are welcome and pay at the outside lounge. Loungers at the poolside.

Outside lounge

9:00 – late

Beside the pool, for drinks and evenings in the open air. Open to hotel guests and visitors.

Inside lounge & restaurant

9:00 – late

An air-conditioned lounge joined to the restaurant, for meals, drinks and meeting friends or colleagues.

Climax Grill

Breakfast every morning

Breakfast is served every morning, with grills and local dishes through the day, in the restaurant or by the pool.

Security & parking

Guarded, gated forecourt

Park on the gated forecourt in front of the hotel. Guards are on duty day and night.

Airport pickup

A driver meets you at arrivals

Tick airport pickup when you book and send your flight number. The front desk confirms the price for your airport when it confirms your room.
