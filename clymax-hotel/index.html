Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Message the front desk on WhatsApp. Someone answers day and night.

Ask the front desk
What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out depend on the room being free, so tell us your arrival time.

How does airport pickup work?

Tick airport pickup when you book and send your flight number. A driver meets you at arrivals. The front desk confirms the price for your airport when it confirms your room.

How do I pay?

At the front desk on arrival, by card, bank transfer or cash. Nothing is paid when you send a booking request.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is served at the Climax Grill every morning. Ask the front desk whether it is included in your rate when you book.

Can I use the pool if I'm not staying?

The pool is for hotel guests. Visitors are welcome at the Climax Grill and in both lounges.

Is there parking and security?

Yes. Parking is on the gated forecourt in front of the hotel, with guards on duty day and night.

Is the Wi-Fi free?

Yes, free and available 24 hours in every room, both lounges and by the pool.
