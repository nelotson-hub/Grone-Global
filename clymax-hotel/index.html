Message the front desk

Is the airport pickup free?

Pickup is arranged for every guest who asks when booking. The front desk confirms the price for your airport when it confirms the room.

Can I use the pool if I'm not staying?

The pool is for hotel guests. Day visitors can eat at the Climax Grill and sit in either lounge.

Is there parking?

Yes, on the gated forecourt in front of the hotel, watched day and night.

Can I check in early?

When the room is ready, yes. Tell us your arrival time and we'll do our best.

How do I pay?

At the front desk on arrival, by card, transfer or cash.
