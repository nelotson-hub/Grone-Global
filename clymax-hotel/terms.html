Terms

A booking request is confirmed only when the front desk replies by email or WhatsApp. Rooms are paid at the front desk on arrival.

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out depend on the room being free.

The pool is for hotel guests. Please tell the front desk about any change to your arrival time or airport pickup as early as you can.
