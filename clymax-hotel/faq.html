Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Message the front desk on WhatsApp. Someone answers day and night.

Ask the front desk
What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 12:00 noon, and check-out is at 12:00 noon the next day. Early check-in and late check-out depend on the room you get, so tell us your arrival time when you book.

How does airport pickup work?

Tick airport pickup when you book and send your flight number. A driver meets you at arrivals. The front desk confirms the price for your airport when it confirms your room.

How do I pay?

At the front desk on arrival, by card, bank transfer or cash. Nothing is paid when you send a booking request, but bookings are confirmed on payment.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is served at the Climax Grill every morning. Ask the front desk whether it is included in your rate when you book.

Can I use the pool if I'm not staying?

Yes. Hotel guests use the pool free of charge, one guest per room. Visitors are welcome too and pay at the outside lounge.

When are the pool and lounges open?

The outdoor pool is open from 8:00 to 20:00. The outside lounge and the inside lounge are open from 9:00 until late.

Is there parking and security?

Yes. Parking is on the gated forecourt in front of the hotel, with guards on duty day and night.

Is the Wi-Fi free?

Yes. Wi-Fi is free and runs 24 hours in every room, both lounges, the restaurant and by the pool.

Is there power all day?

Yes. We have uninterrupted power supply, so air conditioning, lights, Wi-Fi and hot water keep running day and night.

How do I book a room?

Use the booking bar under the video on the home page, the Book this room button on any room, or message us on WhatsApp at +234 707 336 9981. The front desk replies with availability and the rate.
