# Clymax Signature Hotel & Suites

## Product
A hotel with rooms and suites, an outdoor pool with a lounge beside it, an indoor lounge joined to the restaurant, and the Climax Grill. 24-hour Wi-Fi, security on site, gated parking and airport pickup. The website's job is to get bookings and reservation enquiries, mainly through WhatsApp.

## Audience
Business travellers, couples and families booking short stays, often on a phone over mobile data. They expect to message the front desk on WhatsApp rather than fill in long forms.

## Surface
Marketing website, one page (mode: Persuade). The primary action is checking availability or booking a room.

## Brand commitments (pinned by the client)
- Logo: the gold CSH seal with "Clymax Signature Hotel & Suites" in classical capitals. It must appear on the site.
- Layout follows the client's reference video: a full-screen hero with the logo top left, a menu (hamburger) button top right, the hotel name centred, and a booking bar in the middle of the hero; rooms follow.
- The hero is the client's own header video of the hotel front, playing on load and zooming in.
- Every video autoplays, muted, looping, with no controls.
- Media must be small and fast to load.

## Facts still to supply
WhatsApp number, phone, email, street address, Facebook page URL, real room names and prices, opening hours, photos of the pool, lounges and grill.
